# Privacy Policy — Chillpill

**Last updated:** March 2025

Chillpill ("the app") is a screen-time reduction tool that helps you limit time in selected apps by showing a block screen and optional grace period.

## Data collection and sharing

**Chillpill does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data with servers or third parties.**  
All data stays on your device.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device:

- **Settings** — Your chosen wait time, grace period, and whether setup is complete.
- **Restricted apps** — The list of apps you chose to restrict and related options (e.g. whether to show a block again after the grace period).
- **Suggestion preferences** — Which apps you chose to ignore for 7 days or to never suggest again.
- **Usage events** — Local records of block-screen attempts, wait completions, and grace-period events, used only to show statistics (e.g. today’s attempts and time in apps) inside the app.

None of this data is sent off the device. There is no analytics, crash reporting, or advertising in the app.

## Permissions

The app uses:

- **Accessibility Service** — Used only to detect which app is in the foreground. This allows ChillPill to show a wait screen when you open a restricted app and to suggest restricting apps you use frequently. The service does not read, collect, or share any content displayed on your screen.
- **Usage access (Package Usage Stats)** — To know how long apps are in the foreground (for the suggestion popup and statistics) and to sort apps in the app-selection screen.
- **Notifications** — To show the grace-period countdown in the notification shade.
- **Foreground service** — To run the grace-period timer in the background.

These are used only for the app’s features and do not send any data elsewhere.

## Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the "Last updated" date above will be changed. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
